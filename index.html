🖥️ Keep it on your desktop

  1. Open this file in Chrome or Edge, and always use that same browser (your progress is saved inside it).
  2. Chrome: ⋮ menu → Cast, save and share → Create shortcut… and tick Open as window. Edge: ⋮ → Apps → Install this site as an app, if it's offered.
  3. Right-click the new window's taskbar icon and choose Pin to taskbar.
  4. On the Dashboard, press ⛶ Widget mode for a clean full-window view. Press Esc to leave it.

⏰ Half-hour reminder

A nudge at :00 and :30 to jot down what the last half-hour went to on the Time tab. It only rings while Life Login is open in a browser tab, but that tab can be in the background.

🌊 Hyperfocus check
Works from your time blocks, so it pairs well with the reminder and ↻ Still on it. Sleep doesn't count. The nudge has its own sound: one soft low "bloop", while the half-hour reminder rings "ding-ding" three times.

🔒 Sync between devices

Keeps your PC and phone in step. Everything is locked with your passphrase on this device before it's uploaded, so the copy online can't be read without it, not even by Google. The theme, reminders and widget mode stay separate on each device.

💾 Save file

The app keeps your progress inside this browser, which is easy to lose (clearing browser data, switching browsers). Pick a save file and every change is also written to that file on your PC within a second. If the browser ever forgets everything, use Open a save file to get it all back.

Daily backups

Pick a folder, like the backups folder inside your life folder, and the app keeps one dated copy per day in it (for example life-login-2026-11-10.json), updated as you go. It keeps the last 30 days and removes older ones. To go back to a day, use Import a backup and pick that day's file.

How it works

📘 Skills: your CompTIA A+ study page. Today's study says where you are in Professor Messer's course and what to do. Flashcards: tap a card to flip it, then Got it sends it further out (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days) and Missed it brings it back. Log each session with its section number and what's still fuzzy (time goes in the Time tab). The Exam map shows what's on each test and what you've covered. Copy for AI copies your log for Claude to summarize and quiz you.

⏳ Time: tap or drag across half-hour blocks and write what you did, then pick a color from green (productive) to red (dead time). ↻ Still on it fills the empty half-hours since your last block in one tap.

✅ To do: one item always sits in the bar at the top of every tab until you do it, snooze it or let it go. The bar changes its color and spot so you keep noticing it (turn that off in Settings → Look).

🌿 Care: calories, water and other trackers on the left, and one Routines list on the right (every day, a few times a week, and on a schedule, with a calendar of what's due). Each day gets two colors, one for calories and one for routines: red to green the closer you get, and purple once you hit your calorie goal or finish every routine.

📊 Stats: pick Week, Month, Year or All time to see green and dead time, calorie days, to-dos, skill practice and active days, each compared with the stretch before. The weekly review and the activity log live here too.

⏰ Reminder and 🌊 hyperfocus check (Settings → Reminder): a ding-ding at every :00 and :30 to fill in the Time tab, and a softer nudge to drink, eat or move when one thing fills 2 hours in a row.

😀 Emoji boxes: click any emoji box for a drop-down with search and your recent picks.

🔥 Streak: any day you log something keeps it going. Today never breaks it before the day is over. Today is highlighted in amber on every calendar.
